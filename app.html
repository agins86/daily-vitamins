    Backup

    Your vitamins are saved by themselves every time you log. If the app ever loses them, it puts them back on its own.

    On a new phone they come back too, as long as Google backup is turned on in the phone's settings.

    Sends your data as a file to email, Drive or another phone.

    Pick a file you shared before. Anything missing here is added; nothing is removed.

    What do you take?

    Turn off the one you never take. It disappears from the Home page. Your history is kept.

    When your day starts

    Most people can leave this at midnight.

    If you are often still up after midnight, set it to the time you usually get up. Then what you take late at night counts for the same day, not the next one.

    How to use the app

    1. Log oneTap Powders or Tablets, then tap the one you took. It asks how many tablets, or how much powder, and that's it.
    2. Made a mistake?Tap Undo on the message at the bottom. Later on, open Progress and tap the entry in Today's log to change its time or remove it.
    3. More choicesPress and hold a tablet or powder to log it for an earlier time, undo the last one, or change it.
    4. Add a new oneOpen Powders or Tablets and tap “Add a tablet” or “Add a powder”.
    5. See how you are doingOpen Progress for today's total, the calendar and your log. To set the most you want in a day, open Settings and tap Daily target.
    6. RemindersOpen Settings, tap Reminders, then “Add a reminder”.
    7. A button on your home screenPress and hold an empty spot on the phone's home screen, choose Widgets, then Daily Vitamins. The big one shows today's total and your last dose; the small one is just two buttons. Tap Powders or Tablets on it, then the one you took and how many.
